# 18. A close miss is a lead, not a discard

Clayten, 2026-10-01. The pass bar was being used as a bin. That is wrong.

If the sign is right and the miss is small — t under 3 but over 2, one thin year red, n short of 200 on a rule that fires a few times a month — the idea stays. It is an indicator. Alter the knob that missed. Do not write it off because it was off a little.

Already in that bucket, do not drop them:

- Liquidation spike when the crowd is already short. 3-day was red in 2026 on 26 trades. 7-day hold is +7.38%, t 4.00, and 2026 is +0.51%.
- Volume climax on a down day. t 5.21. 2026 is the only red year, and 62% of the days are the liquidation buy.
- Predicted funding below realized. t 4.81. Fails 2022, flat in 2025.
- Low funding long. t 2.20.
- Level-break chase. t 1.20, n 118. The quiet version is the one that failed, not the trend version.

A loss on the only version tested can still be dropped, and the file has to say which version. A near miss cannot.
