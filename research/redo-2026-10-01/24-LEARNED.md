# 24. What the contrary results say — new hypotheses

These are not book adds. Each one is the idea the failed test actually supported. A later session tests the hypothesis, not the original wording.

1. A break with open interest jumping is continuation, not a fade. The short lost. The long in a trend or expanded volatility was the side that paid. Falsify it if the next year of trend breaks loses after the fee.

2. Extreme funding is momentum, not a top. Shorting the 95th lost in every regime. Falsify it if a year appears where the extreme-funding short is green after a one-day delay and a 7-day hold.

3. A liquidation spike is a buy, and only when it is wide. One coin firing is noise. Four or more, with the crowd already short, held 7 days, is the spec in `22-WASHOUT-SPEC.md`. Falsify it if the next 30 closed paper trades are red.

4. Catch-up rotation is false. The group that already went up keeps going. Majors' own momentum beat the hand-off. Falsify it if a full year appears where laggards beat the leaders after a week.

5. A quiet crowd-high short is a bear-year lean, not a rule. It paid in 2020 and 2022 and lost 2024. Falsify it if it pays in the next expansion year.

6. Spot leading a rally is continuation. Perp leading a rally is not a short. Falsify the spot side if the next two years are red. The perp short is already red on every alteration run.

7. A volume climax on a down day is the same family as the liquidation buy. It pays when four or more coins do it, and 62% of those days are already the liquidation spike. Falsify it if the volume days with no liquidation spike start paying on their own at t of 3.

8. A lone coin at a 20-day high is not a buy. Many coins at a high is the bull drift, not a signal. Falsify it if a single-coin 20-day high turns green across a bear year and a bull year.

9. Predicted funding below realized is a long, not a short. The short of either series lost. Falsify the long if 2022-like years keep failing after the crowd-low filter.
