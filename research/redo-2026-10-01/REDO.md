### What altering changed
Shorting the squeeze, shorting the crowd, and shorting a perp-led rally do not get saved by a delay, a coin list, or a second filter. The squeeze short is worse after a run-up. The crowd short only turns up in compressed vol, and that turn is two years. The perp-led short gets worse after a run-up. The working alteration on each is the other side, which is the book already running: buy the forced unwind, do not sell it.
