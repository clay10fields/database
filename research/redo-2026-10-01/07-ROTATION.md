# 7. Coin-group rotation — lead, not a book add

Read `REDO.md` for the legend. This file is the walk that REDO.md had marked as only a glance.

Idea. When majors beat alts this week, buy the laggard group next week. The catch-up is the trade.

Rule. Weekly close-to-close. Majors are the average of BTC and ETH. Alts are the average of SOL, ADA, AVAX, DOT, LINK, LTC, BCH. Memes are DOGE and SHIB. If majors beat alts this week, long the alt basket next week. Fee 0.10% once. Data: `raw/coinalyze_daily/perp_ohlcv.csv`, weeks from 2019-09-09 to 2026-10-04. One basket week is one trade, so t is not clustered further.

Result. After majors beat alts, long alts next week: 194 weeks, +1.71%, t 2.20.

What was changed. The other way, alts beat majors then long majors: 174 weeks, +0.31%, t 0.43. That side does not pay. After alts beat majors, long memes: 142 weeks, +2.45%, t 0.90. Too noisy.

The control is own momentum, not rotation. Alts up this week, long alts next week: 178 weeks, +1.87%, t 2.17. Majors up this week, long majors next week: 197 weeks, +1.76%, t 2.88. Meme own momentum: 146 weeks, +5.82%, t 2.06. The rotation does not beat just holding the group that already went up.

Years, rotation long alts. 2020 +4.02 (29). 2021 +7.77 (19). 2022 -2.86 (32). 2023 +3.41 (29). 2024 +1.87 (30). 2025 -0.39 (33). 2026 +0.72 (22). The bear year loses. The last two years do not pay.

Verdict. Lead. Fails t of 3, and 2022 and 2025 fail. It is not better than own momentum of the majors. Do not add it. Do not cite the earlier glance as the result. This file is the walk.
