# 25. Next hypotheses — built from what failed

The original idea was the short. The data supported the other side. These are the hypotheses to test next. Each has a rule, the evidence already in hand, and the one test that would kill it.

H1. Wide washout long. Long the close when long-liquidations are at the coin's own 95th, the crowd is at its own 10th, and at least four coins spiked. Hold 7 days. Evidence: 141 trades, +7.00%, t 3.00, both halves positive, 2026 +4.20% on 15. Kill it if the next 30 closed paper trades are red. This is the one already specified.

H2. Break continuation, not fade. Long a break of the prior 20-day high when open interest jumps, only in trend or expanded volatility, hold 24 hours. Evidence: the short lost, the long was t 1.20 on 118 in the 4-hour panel. Kill it if a year of trend breaks is red after the fee. Not tested on the daily file yet.

H3. Funding is momentum. Do not short a funding spike. The long is funding at its own 5th only when the coin is already up on the week. Evidence: the short was t about -5. The plain long was t 2.20 and failed 2025 and 2026. Kill the long if the week-up filter does not lift 2025 and 2026 above zero.

H4. Own momentum, not catch-up. Long the major that was already up last week, not the alt that lagged. Evidence: majors' own momentum t 2.88, the hand-off was negative. Kill it if a full year of laggards beats the leaders after a week.

H5. Spot-led continuation. Long a day the spot tape leads the rally. Do not short a perp-led rally. Evidence: spot-led long t 3.01, perp-led short red on every alteration. Kill the long if the next two years are red.

H6. Predicted-funding gap long. Long when predicted funding is below realized, not the short of either. Evidence: t 4.81 on 3,438, fails 2022, flat 2025. Kill it if the crowd-low filter does not repair 2022.

None of these replace the current book. H1 is the paper spec. H2 through H6 are the ones the failed shorts left behind.
