Method

How the grid is built

  • Regime is one market clock taken from BTC, with no look-ahead. Stress: BTC 20-bar volatility above its trailing 250-bar 90th percentile. Trend up or down: BTC 30-bar efficiency ratio above 0.35, signed by the 30-bar move. Calm: everything else. Each state must last 3 bars.
  • Every variable is ranked against that coin's own trailing 90 days and split into quarters, so a meme coin's crowd reading is compared with its own history, not with BTC's.
  • "Vs normal" is the cell's average minus the average of every bar for the same group and regime. No fees. This is a map, not a trading rule.
  • t is cluster-robust by calendar week, because 72h windows overlap and coins move together. Cells with fewer than 30 bars are hidden; cells under 300 bars are dimmed.
  • Source: research/coin-types-2026-10-01/grid.py in the database repo.
