## 0. The one-paragraph objective

Clayten is building a research instrument, not a bot. The question it must answer: **for the 16 perps
tradeable on Kraken US / Kalshi (BTC ETH SOL XRP ADA DOGE LTC DOT LINK AAVE AVAX BCH HBAR SHIB XLM XTZ),
which market regime are we in, how do the leverage variables (OI, funding, liquidations, spot vs perp
taker flow, long/short ratio) behave and co-move inside each regime, and what is the highest-expectancy
trade in each regime — with a pre-written backup plan for when the primary trade fails that minimizes loss
or flips to profit.** Everything in this repo exists to make that answer measurable instead of argued.

Clayten's working hypothesis (the "Squeeze Toolkit"): OI, funding, liquidations and spot flow are
*symptoms* that point direction when price breaks a level. Your job is to find the conditions where that
holds — work to support it, not just to refute it — while keeping every number straight. He does not write
code; you do. He wants execution, not questions. If a step can be done, do it.

## 3. What we found today (all on 4h bars, 16 coins, 2025-11 → 2026-09, fees 0.1% round trip)

Full detail with numbers: `research/squeeze-2026-09-30/trade_playbook.md` (read the UPDATE blocks at the bottom
first — they supersede the top), `coin_ranking.md`, `squeeze_parameter_sweep.xlsx` (17,850-row grid, Lookup sheet
keyed `rule|scope|thr|off|read|hold`), `sweep_results.csv`, `spotflow_events_7coins.csv`.

**Rule A_fade (the core finding):** first 4h close above the prior UTC day's high, AND open interest at the bar
after the break is >4% above OI at the bar before (an 8h read) → SHORT at the close of the bar after the break,
hold 36h. Enter at offset 0.
- POOLED 10 good coins (ADA DOGE XRP SOL AVAX ETH LTC HBAR LINK SHIB): **+1.80%/trade**. Shuffle test p≈0.
- POOLED 16: +0.29% (first half +2.14%, second half −1.73% — the September rallies).
- Coin verdicts: STRONG ADA DOGE XRP SOL AVAX ETH LTC HBAR SHIB · weak LINK · OPPOSITE (fade loses) BTC XLM DOT
  AAVE BCH XTZ.
- Dose is a step, not a slope: OI jump 4–8% works; 2–3% is negative; 8–12% dead (too few). 8h read beats 4h read.
  Fixed 4% beats z-score (lookahead) and rolling quantiles. Volatility-expanding is a booster, not a gate.
- Mirror rules (chase/continuation, long on low-break bounce) do not clear fees on the pooled set.

**Exit rule:** at +8h, if price is >1% against the short AND OI is still rising → cover. Changes the trades it
touches from −4.3% to roughly flat; whole-book effect +0.44% → +0.98%.

**Spot-flow filter (Clayten's hypothesis, confirmed in direction):** on the break bar, compute spot taker net
buy share `net = (2·sbv − sv)/sv` and `surge = sv / trailing-7-day-mean(sv)`.
- 100 signals across BCH DOT AAVE ADA DOGE XRP ETH. net>+0.10 → 12 signals, −2.5%. surge>4× → 9 signals, −5.5%.
  Everything else → 80 signals, +1.5%, 66% win, t=2.3. corr(net, fade P&L) = −0.42.
- **Filter B (adopted): skip the short if net > +10% or surge > 4× on the break bar.** Costs nothing on good coins
  (ADA, XRP skip 0; DOGE skips 4 at +0.9%; ETH 1), turns BCH from −1.9% to +1.0%. AAVE/DOT still negative → off.
- Filter B + 8h exit on all 100: **+1.49%, t=2.7**. Second half of sample (trend month): −1.63% → +0.04%.
  It stops the bleeding; it does not make a trending month profitable.
- The break bar alone carries the signal (corr −0.40 vs −0.42 with two bars) — no need to wait.
- "The last hour" cannot be tested from history — hourly spot flow older than ~2 months does not exist free.
  That is exactly why the recorder exists.

**What failed:** z-score thresholds (lookahead), rolling-quantile thresholds, chase/continuation rules, flip-to-long
on failed fades (only works on trending coins, i.e., only in hindsight), tighter spot filter (net≤0.15 AND
surge≤3× — threw away 13 winners on good coins).

**Honest caveats:** 11 months; 16 coins with pairwise correlation ~0.5–0.7 ≈ 1.5–2.5 independent series;
~47 independent trades needed for t≈2 on a +1.75% mean with 6% sd; trades firing in the same 8h window across coins
are one cluster, not N. Second-half weakness is real. Treat everything as "not rejected," not "proven."

## 5. What the literature says (research/evidence-review/EVIDENCE-REVIEW-2026-09-30.md)

Read it in full; the short version that changes what you build:
- Evidence supports **three regimes at most**: A normal/rotation, B directional trend, C stress/liquidation.
  Volatility is the only regime axis with strong, replicated support. Grok's five regimes and the ADX 20/25,
  ATR 0.85/1.30 constants have no out-of-sample crypto evidence. Volume-profile auction states are untestable
  without volume-at-price data.
- Regime persistence on BTC ≈ 20–27 days → ~12–16 regime spells in our 11 months, shared across all coins.
- Funding is a crash-risk gauge at weeks-to-month horizon (BIS: 10% carry rise → short liquidations +44% of OI next
  month), **not** a 4h–3d direction signal (Presto R²≈0; pre-registered carry-fade null after costs).
- "Liquidation flush = bottom" is false on average (K33: +0.78% 30d vs +5.9% normal; only ~¼ are bottoms).
- **No published forward-return statistics exist for price×OI quadrants or spot-led vs perp-led breakouts.
  Our data is ahead of the literature on both.** The OI-jump fade is the top validation priority.
- Our +8h/−1% exit is theory-backed (stops add value exactly when momentum is present — a failing fade is evidence
  of momentum). Default to flatten, not reverse: best published continuation edge (+15 bps next day, 49% win) ≈ fees.
- Taker-flow-driven moves mean-revert (strong, but at 15 min the edge is below costs); real/spot order flow has
  persistent effects (~10% of daily, 20% of weekly return variance). That is the mechanism behind filter B.
- Sizing: vol targeting, fractional Kelly ≤0.25×, kill switches in regime C (BTC realized vol >90th pct or
  liquidations >95th pct → flatten fades).
