SERIES OVERVIEW
- 100 Series: budget-friendly, standard sizes, double-hung/casement/picture/gliding
- 200 Series: Narroline gliding, tilt-wash, higher-end standard line
- 400 Series: full-frame, custom sizes, Flexiframe specialty shapes, Frenchwood patio doors
- A-Series: premium, custom shapes, wood/aluminum clad

NOTES
- 100 Series half-circle windows changed manufacturing spec Oct 2025 (see parts catalog)
- Custom shapes available in 1/8 inch increments on many series
- Contact local Andersen supplier for dealer pricing and lead times
